# Helping out

Thanks for wanting to help. Here's how.

## Found a bug?

[Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) and pick "Something broke". Say what you did, what happened, and paste the error. The error shows on the page, or in the black window the app runs in.

## Have an idea?

Open an issue and pick "Idea". Say what you'd like the app to do, and why it would help you.

## Want to change the code?

1. Fork this repo and make your change.
2. Keep it simple: plain Python and plain JavaScript, no build step, no new frameworks.
3. Test it with a fresh copy of the folder (no `.env`, no data), so you see what a new person sees. Try at least one of the three AI options.
4. Open a pull request. Say what you changed and why, in a few lines.

Never commit a `.env` file, a `cookies.txt`, or anyone's transcripts.
